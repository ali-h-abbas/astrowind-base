This Privacy Policy describes how Amor Fati ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use this website.

## Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information. The primary data collected is:

- **Usage Data** — standard web analytics such as pages visited, time on page, browser type, and device information, collected automatically when you visit the site.
- **Email address** — only if you voluntarily provide it when subscribing or enrolling in a program.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate and improve the website
- Send course-related communications if you have enrolled
- Respond to inquiries you submit to us

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Cookies

This website may use cookies to understand how visitors use the site (e.g., via Google Analytics). You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function correctly as a result.

## Third-Party Services

Enrollment and payment processing is handled by Stripe. Please review [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for information on how they handle your data.

## Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

## Your Rights
